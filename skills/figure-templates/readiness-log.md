### Readiness continuation log

2026-11-17 (third pass — critique fixes): `elevation_scene` + `two_circle_geometry` integrated (solved sightline scenes; computed touch/intersect chord geometry). Post-critique contract fixes: an explicit `foot` letter on a touch pair now refuses (default `'M'` applies to intersecting pairs only), `paired_geometry` refuses unknown scaffold-spec keys, `altitude=True` refuses on the right prism profile, and the catalogue row lists `triangle_aas`. 2026-11-17 (second pass): `outcome_grid` gains `events` — per-event true cell-set contours (oval stadium / full-block box / single circle, split regions, label arrows; non-member enclosure and duplicate contours refuse) plus whole-number/bool index validation; new templates `fraction_tape` (aligned strips + braces, tank water level), `area_model` (symbolic 2×2 algebra partition, no drawn vertex letters) and `circular_composite` (seven bounded profiles: rect+arc pieces, stadium, triangle+semicircle, sector pieces, cut-and-attach semicircles, annulus and nested-square frame — numeric or symbolic dims). The outcome_grid superset is byte-identical on the pre-existing self-test specs; `circular_composite` does its own finite-first numeric validation (it is deliberately NOT inside `_validate_numeric_inputs`, which would reject symbolic dims).

2026-11-17: G10 additions — solid gains `half_cylinder` and profiled triangular prisms (right/isosceles/scalene, altitude, open trough); net gains canonical triangular-prism and cylinder nets; `dot_pattern` gains `kind="rings"`; new templates `motion_states`, `slat_frame`, `clock_face`, `pairing_arcs`, `paired_geometry` (multi-scaffold composed marked geometry). New dims are numeric-only; legacy solid/net delegation is byte-identical. Critics' fixes baked in: scalene near-endpoint foot refuses, `X` reserved whenever an altitude anchor is written, right-profile X↔B duplicate anchors removed, altitude ink carries a `describe`, and "an isosceles" grammar.

2026-11-16: Added explicit figure-reading modes for inscribed-circle tangent labels, Venn region tokens, composite-solid dimensions and square-pyramid net dimensions. Earlier stem-only catalogue entries above are superseded by these mode contracts where applicable. Two-circle tangents and both construction stages remain intentionally stem-only because they do not print numeric givens.
